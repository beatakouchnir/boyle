Lossless MoE Streaming on Apple Silicon
The measured map, experiments E1–E17 · M5 Max, 128 GB · Aug 2026

The question: how fast can mixture-of-experts models larger than memory run on a consumer Apple-silicon machine, with outputs bit-identical to a resident model? The answer, measured to closure: a 397 GB-class model decodes at ~8.8 tok/s and a 132 GB model at ~15.5 on 128 GB of RAM; under the GPU's wired ceiling, streaming is free (resident speed at every bit-rate); above it, one law governs everything — and every remaining millisecond now sits at a measured floor.

1 · The two regimes: the wired wall divides the world

Metal wires the entire bytesNoCopy buffer on first kernel use (a 3 MB one-expert gather faults its full 1.2 GB tensor), and the wired ceiling counts mapped buffers — a 217 GB model demand-mapped through stock kernels dies at ~112 GB touched (kIOGPUCommandBufferCallbackErrorOutOfMemory). Transparent bigger-than-RAM GPU streaming therefore cannot exist on this platform. Two architectures survive, one per regime:

2 · The capacity law (three families, one curve)

Routing is flat in every family measured — gemma-lineage (128 experts), Qwen (512), DeepSeek-lineage GLM (256, sigmoid + shared expert): LFU ≪ LRU at every budget, clairvoyant OPT only +0.07 hit over LRU, per-domain expert unions covering 42–46% of all (layer, expert) pairs. No hot set exists. Consequently over-wall speed is a function of two numbers only: the budget-to-expert-mass ratio (→ hit rate, via one reusable curve) and bytes-per-miss. A trace-driven simulator built on that curve predicted live hit rates within 1–3 points on four model/quant configurations.

5 · Thinking-mode economics under streaming (E14)

Per-token throughput is mode-blind — thinking ran 37% faster per token (cache warmth) — but costs 6× the tokens: 3.4–4.4× slower per answer, ≥4.3× worse time-per-correct even crediting all censored items (gsm8k, n=100/mode; accuracy comparison remains censored-open: 0.96 answer-mode vs [0.84, 0.97] thinking). Corollary adopted program-wide: short-generation cells understate steady-state speed; long rungs joined the standard design.

6 · Method — what kept this honest

  • Pre-registration: every experiment declared predictions and decision rules before data. Three predictions were beaten (identical batching 6.2×, E17b −47%), several confirmed (capacity rule, GLM at 1.72 in a pre-declared band), and five hypotheses killed by their own written criteria.
  • Refuse-to-fake asserts: wrap counts, mechanism counters, sentinel checks — two A/B rounds were voided by silently-inactive mechanisms and caught only by counting invocations (0 and 0).
  • Bit-identity as the correctness bar everywhere the contract promises it, at three quant tiers.
  • Physical verification over accounting: per-process ledgers are blind to mapped pages; msync produces accounting-only "pseudo-eviction"; the only honest eviction test is a timed re-read.
  • Measured noise floor (~1.5% step-time), so nulls are nulls.
